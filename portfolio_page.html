Key Findings

  • Email leads every campaign format on click-through rate at 5.25%, more than 4x Native Display's 1.25%.
  • The escalation-risk model's top decile concentrates 13.42% actual escalation versus 1.62% overall, an 8.27x lift at 0.954 test AUC.
  • The duplicate-row check catches 2,450 rows sharing an identical business key -- a double-loaded feed defect caught before it reaches a client.
  • Oncology returns the strongest attributed ROI at 7.18x spend, more than double Endocrinology's 3.46x.
  • Aggregate pacing tracks within 1.50% of contract, but 9.55% of individual reports still breach the 80-120% tolerance band.

Recommendations

Immediate (0-30 Days)

Work the decile-1 escalation queue first
Same-day QA review on the model's highest-priority reports before they reach a client.
Fix the double-loaded feed
Deduplicate the 2,450 report rows sharing an identical business key.

Short-Term (30-90 Days)

Target the lowest-pacing clients
Use the pacing-by-client ranking to get ahead of the client's own questions.
Reallocate underperforming targeting
Shift budget away from the lowest-CTR specialty x region cells.

Strategic (90+ Days)

Embed the QA score at generation time
Convert the checkpoint from a downstream pass into a pre-send control.
Run the model as a daily queue-ranking job
Keep triage current as new reports land, not as a one-time batch score.
